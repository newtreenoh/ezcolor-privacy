EzColor Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026

This policy covers the EzColor app on Android, iOS and the web, and this privacy page. EzColor is provided by Newtreenoh. Your artwork is stored on your device or in your browser. Signing in lets you use account features and access eligible purchases across supported devices.

Information we handle

  • Artwork and preferences. Drawings, edits, imports, project names, favourites and preferences are stored in the app on your device or in the storage for your browser profile. EzColor processes the files you choose to import on your device. EzColor does not upload your artwork or sync it to your account. When you export a file or an editable backup, the destination you choose controls that copy.
  • Sign-in and account. If you sign in, Google or Apple provides Firebase Authentication with information such as your provider account identifier, email address and any profile details it supplies, such as your display name. Apple may provide a private relay email address. EzColor uses your account identifier and authentication token to verify sign-in, check access to online features and process account deletion. Your sign-in provider handles your password.
  • Purchases and subscriptions. Google Play or the App Store processes checkout in the mobile app. EzColor receives the product identifier and a purchase token or transaction reference. We verify the purchase with the store and record its reference, status, expiry where applicable, and a generated EzColor account identifier. We use this information to grant and restore access, recognise purchases on web, and account for renewals, cancellations and refunds. The store handles your payment details.
  • Technical information. Online services may process your IP address, browser or device information and request details to deliver the service and protect it against misuse. Firebase can use IP addresses and browser information for sign-in security. The web app uses browser storage for artwork and settings and session storage to keep you signed in. This privacy page uses no scripts, analytics or tracking cookies of its own.
  • Support correspondence. If you email us, we use your email address, the contents of your message and any information you choose to send to respond, verify a request or resolve a problem.

Where UK or EU data protection law applies, we use account and purchase information as necessary to provide the features you request and fulfil your purchases. We rely on our legitimate interests to deliver and secure online services, prevent fraud and handle support, and on legal obligations when the law requires processing or retention.

Delete your EzColor account and data

In the app

While signed in, open Settings, select Account and plans and choose Delete account. Confirm the request and sign in again with the same provider to verify it.

By email

You can request deletion without the app. Email gpc.immerse077@passinbox.com with the subject EzColor account deletion request. Include the email address used for your EzColor account, including your Apple relay address if you used Hide My Email. We may ask you to verify that the account is yours. Keep passwords, verification codes and payment card details out of your message.

What happens when you delete your account

We delete your EzColor sign-in record and the linked purchase and access records that we control. This removes the account's access to paid features. Google and Apple manage their own accounts, transaction records and subscriptions. If you have an active subscription, cancel it separately through the store where you bought it to stop future renewals.

To remove local artwork, delete projects in My Art. To remove all local app data, clear EzColor’s app data on Android, delete the iOS app, or clear EzColor’s site data in your browser. Delete any copies or backups you exported separately from the places where you saved them.

Who receives information

  • Google provides Firebase Authentication, Google Sign-In and Google Play Billing. Firebase privacy and security · Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple where offered and handles App Store purchases. Apple Privacy Policy
  • GitHub hosts this policy and may handle technical information about your visit. GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Hosting and email providers process information as needed to deliver EzColor’s online services and handle correspondence. We may also disclose information when legally required.

We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising. EzColor does not use advertising or behavioural tracking in the app.

Providers may process information in countries outside your own. Where UK or EU transfer rules apply, we rely on applicable safeguards such as an adequacy decision or approved contractual safeguards. You can contact us for information about safeguards that apply to your data.

How we protect and retain information

EzColor communicates with online services over HTTPS. Sign-in is required for access to account and purchase records. We check that the account is yours before processing a deletion request.

Local projects and preferences remain in your app or browser storage until you delete them or that storage is cleared. Keep your own editable backup if you want to preserve your artwork after changing devices or clearing storage.

We keep sign-in and verified purchase records while your account is active so that you can sign in and restore purchases. We remove them when your account is deleted, subject to any limited information we need to keep to comply with the law, prevent fraud or resolve disputes. We keep support correspondence for as long as needed to handle the request and any related obligations. The providers listed above set their own retention periods for the records they control. Firebase explains its authentication backup and IP address retention in its privacy and security documentation.

Your rights and how to contact us

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, receive a copy of it, or restrict or object to certain uses. Contact gpc.immerse077@passinbox.com for privacy questions, requests or concerns. If you are a parent or guardian concerned about a child's information, you can reach us at the same address.

You can also complain to your local data protection authority. In the UK, that is the Information Commissioner’s Office.

We publish changes to this policy on this page and update the effective date.
